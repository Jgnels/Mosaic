# Mosaic Typed Relationship Pilot V3 Audit

## Verdict

`TYPED_GROUNDING_PASS_RENDERING_QUALITY_LIMITING_RESULT`

V3 completed 12/12 shadow-only calls with no canonical mutation. Typed-contract acceptance,
positive-only trust, and mixed-history mixed disposition were all 1.00. Model-authored dialogue was
0.00. Exact request archives passed credential and internal-condition scans.

The typed contract prevented fabricated citations, valence mismatches, inconsistent dispositions,
and provider-authored motive or personality claims. This directly addresses V2's grounding failure.

The deterministic renderer exposed a separate product limitation: legacy evidence summaries such as
"warned Rowan before a raid" become awkward after "because." This is not a grounding failure, but it
reduces believability and player value.

Decision: close the provider gate. Graduate typed claims, then implement event-kind-aware grammatical
rendering offline before considering another provider protocol.
