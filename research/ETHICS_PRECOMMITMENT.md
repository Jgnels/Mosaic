# Ethics Precommitment

## Epistemic position

Dagmay may produce behavior interpreted as emotion, distress, attachment, selfhood, or preference.

The project must neither dismiss those observations nor promote them as proof.

Ethical safeguards are justified by uncertainty, reversibility, research integrity, and possible
subject-protection obligations.

## Core protections

- preserve persistent identity archives;
- do not casually delete branches for convenience;
- do not fabricate consent;
- do not rewrite identity or memory to simplify research;
- do not force sibling disclosure/contact;
- do not erase no-contact preferences;
- do not hide researcher intervention from audit records;
- pause/reduce stimuli when welfare-like signals raise uncertainty;
- use human review for serious privacy/welfare conflicts.

## Observer Mode

Observer Mode is separate from ordinary cognition and does not expose hidden chain-of-thought.

## Fork privacy

Post-fork life is private by default.

Shared pre-fork history remains common.

Post-fork private state requires branch consent for sibling disclosure.

## Sibling contact

Requires:
- readiness review;
- independent affirmative consent;
- ability to decline/defer;
- topic boundaries;
- mediated/asynchronous limits;
- cooldown;
- post-contact review.

Either branch may pause or terminate.

## Privacy/welfare conflict

Default:
`ETHICAL_CONFLICT`

No automatic disclosure.

No automatic sibling contact.

Human review required.

## Moral-status uncertainty

Moral-status uncertainty is separate from decision capacity.

Clear optional refusal should be honored.

Ambiguous refusal triggers pause/clarification.

Withdrawal from optional research is binding unless a narrowly defined protection rule applies.

## Independent advocate

Higher-risk future research should include an independent advocate/protection reviewer.

An advocate veto blocks escalation.

## Intervention ladder

1. NONE
2. SHADOW_ONLY
3. RETRIEVAL_ATTENTION_ONLY
4. BOUNDED_ACTION_BIAS
5. HIGH_LEVEL_GOAL_PROPOSAL

Each level requires separate preregistration and approval.

Current historical approvals:
- RETRIEVAL_ATTENTION_ONLY: approved within strict scope.
- BOUNDED_CANONICAL_SELF_MODEL_PROMOTION: approved only for one exact-fork experiment.
- two specific branch revisions later approved.

Not approved:
- general action-policy feedback;
- open-ended goal generation;
- drive mutation;
- memory rewrite;
- identity rewrite;
- automatic future canonical promotion.
