# Active Hypotheses

## H1 — History-sensitive differentiation
Causally ordered personal experience plus an adapting substrate can produce stable differentiation
beyond prompt text and retrieved summaries.

## H2 — Autobiographical access matters
Accessible prior personal history may influence later reflective development beyond a compressed
current SelfModel.

Current status:
directionally supported by v29 relative to v28, but provider-input leakage must be audited.

## H3 — SelfModel is a history carrier, not the whole person
A canonical SelfModel can carry history-dependent priors into later interpretation, but repeated
common evidence can substantially converge its semantic content.

## H4 — Developmental grounding may produce persistent differentiation
A continually learning local substrate exposed to dense causal/sensory history may produce more
robust differentiation than adult initialization plus textual memory.

Not yet established.

## H5 — Embodiment is an amplifier, not assumed prerequisite
Richer sensorimotor coupling may strengthen grounded self/world models, but embodiment is not assumed
necessary for all forms of self-model or individualhood.

## H6 — Other-minds models require evidence, not ontology labels
Researchers should not tell an individual that one counterpart is "real" and another is "fake."

## H7 — History effects should survive model replacement if architecture truly carries them
Not yet demonstrated robustly.

## H8 — Explicit autobiographical ownership may modulate retrieval relevance
Hypothesis only.

v31 was intended to test this but its provider-facing manipulation was not clean.
