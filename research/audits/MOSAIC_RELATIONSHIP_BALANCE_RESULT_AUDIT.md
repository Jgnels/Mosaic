# Mosaic Relationship Balance Pilot — Result Audit

**SUPPORTED:** the model followed the TRUST/MIXED distinction and cited helpful and harmful events
in all six mixed histories.

**LIMITING RESULT:** valid citations did not ensure every clause was entailed. One response inferred
"true intentions," which the events did not establish.

Mosaic must therefore separate citation validity, evidence balance, and claim entailment. Only the
first two passed. Provider dialogue does not graduate to canonical RimWorld behavior until the third
is validated.
