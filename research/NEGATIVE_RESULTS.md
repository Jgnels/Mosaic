# Negative and Limiting Results

## NR-001 — Volatility-prior meta-transfer failed
The initial meta-learning transfer mechanism did not outperform scratch learning.

Do not claim current Dagmay learns how to learn across worlds from this mechanism.

## NR-002 — Basic external learner failed harder MiniGrid tasks
DoorKey/FourRooms performance did not establish compositional competence.

## NR-003 — Fixed-layout FourRooms success did not transfer cleanly
Strong within-layout learning did not establish layout-invariant structural generalization.

## NR-004 — DoorKey affordance composition unresolved
Candidate missing mechanisms:
- object affordances;
- subgoals;
- procedural skill composition;
- multi-step planning;
- stronger state abstraction.

## NR-005 — Schema-balanced LLM causal perspective discovery failed
v12 matched-schema low-level time-series perspective identification was unreliable.

Lesson:
causal learning belongs in an explicit local substrate, not assumed from the LLM.

## NR-006 — Early retrieval-attention designs had ceiling/bug issues
v15 exposed baseline-prevalence ceiling and design problems.

## NR-007 — Lexical distance conflicted with semantic convergence
v25 showed increasing lexical divergence while substantive semantic propositions converged.

## NR-008 — Residual branch signal did not exceed trajectory variation
v28 six-trajectory experiment:
history grouping unresolved relative to stochastic reflective trajectories.

## NR-009 — v31 attribution-framing causal claim invalid
The real provider-facing payload did not implement clean source-relation withholding.

## NR-010 — Condition-label leakage risk
Semantic branch names may have leaked experimental condition information through provider-facing
subject IDs.

Affected provider experiments require input audit before strong causal interpretation.
