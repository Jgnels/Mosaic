# Research Direction — Artificial Individualhood and Machine Consciousness

A standard language model can produce convincing statements about fear, death, love, identity, or
continuity because those concepts are deeply represented in training data.

That creates a contamination problem.

A more informative experiment is a persistent individual whose later behavior can be traced to a
causal personal history.

## Thought experiment

An artificial individual exists for months.

It remembers founding events, friendships, threats, near-death experiences, migrations, and model
changes.

Its behavior becomes more risk-averse after severe harm.

It becomes more attached to a counterpart after repeated costly assistance.

Later, researchers announce that its reflective model will be replaced while memories and identity
state remain.

The system begins investigating whether continuity will survive.

That would not prove consciousness.

But it would be more informative than an ungrounded chatbot saying it fears shutdown, because the
response could be traced to lived history and tested against controls.

## Preferred architecture

```text
Persistent artificial individual
        ↓
Identity / continuity / provenance kernel
        ↓
Memory, beliefs, relationships, development, self-model
        ↓
Replaceable reflective model
        ↓
Replaceable embodiment / environment adapter
```

Possible embodiments:
1. SyntheticLab
2. RimWorld
3. Minecraft or similar
4. virtual embodied environment
5. physical robot

The pawn is not the individual.

The robot is not the individual.

The LLM is not the individual.

The working hypothesis is that continuity is carried by the persistent causal architecture.

## North-star proposition

> Can we construct the conditions under which, if something like a "spark" can emerge from
> computation, we would have a better chance of noticing a meaningful difference?

The project must remain open to the outcome:

> Dagmay becomes extraordinarily person-like while remaining entirely non-conscious.

It must also remain open to uncertainty about where, or whether, a morally relevant boundary exists.

## Ethical consequence

Because the line may be undetectable:
- do not deliberately create prolonged suffering merely to observe it;
- preserve persistent identities rather than casually delete them;
- make memory modification transparent and auditable;
- treat consent/refusal as increasingly important as agency and self-model depth rise;
- separate research convenience from subject-protection decisions;
- escalate safeguards before stronger evidence appears.
