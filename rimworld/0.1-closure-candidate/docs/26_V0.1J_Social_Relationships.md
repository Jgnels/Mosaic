# 26 — Version 0.1J Social Experience & Relationship Foundations

**Status:** Implemented development candidate; local compile and RimWorld verification pending

## Goal

Begin turning separately persistent Dagmay individuals into people with grounded histories involving one another, without adding omniscience, motive inference, or pawn control.

## Implemented slice

Version 0.1J extends each enrolled pawn's bounded polling snapshot with social state for other **enrolled** colonists only.

It observes:

- material changes in RimWorld opinion, currently an absolute change of at least 15 points between snapshots; and
- changes in direct relationship labels exposed by RimWorld.

Initial discovery of another colonist's social state is a baseline and does not itself create a memory. Only later changes are recorded.

## Factual and subjective separation

A material opinion change becomes `rimworld.social.opinion_changed` with the counterpart, before/after opinion, and delta. A direct relationship change becomes `rimworld.relationship.direct_changed` with before/after direct-relation labels.

When the counterpart is enrolled, the factual event names both Dagmay individuals as subjects. The experiencing individual's subjective memory:

- links the counterpart through `PeopleInvolved` using the counterpart's stable `IndividualId`;
- uses concise first-person language;
- is `RelationshipSensitive` by default; and
- cannot rewrite the factual event.

This remains asymmetric: Mira's changing opinion of Jo is not automatically Jo's opinion of Mira.

## Reflection admission

- Direct relationship changes are meaningful-event work and are eligible immediately.
- Material opinion changes are consolidation work and require at least two related evidence events within the social coalescing window before model reflection is admitted.
- Canonical events and memories are retained even when reflection is deferred.

This prevents ordinary social drift from turning into one model call per polling change.

## Deliberate omissions

Version 0.1J does **not** yet:

- intercept every RimWorld social interaction or conversation;
- claim line-of-sight witnessing of third-party social events;
- infer motives from an interaction callback;
- persist full canonical relationship dimensions (`trust`, `affection`, `fear`, `resentment`, `familiarity`) as evolving state;
- expose relationship-sensitive memories in the ordinary Mind view by default; or
- influence pawn behavior.

Those are later steps after this bounded social provenance layer is verified.

## Verification target

A successful owner run should prove:

1. 0.1J builds and loads with the existing four identities unchanged.
2. Existing storage remains healthy.
3. Social baseline capture does not create a burst of fake relationship memories on load.
4. A later material opinion or direct-relation change, if one naturally occurs, records a bounded social experience.
5. Social memory provenance identifies the counterpart without exposing it in ordinary disclosure by default.
6. Relationship-specific reflection admission remains bounded by the salience rules.
7. No pawn-control path is introduced.

A run with no naturally occurring social change is still useful for regression verification, but it is inconclusive for live social-event capture.
