## 0.1L — Persistence Torture

**Isolation harness implemented:** `tools\run-persistence-torture.ps1` runs six deterministic save/reload cycles and writes `artifacts\Dagmay-persistence-torture-latest.json`. It covers:
- queued reflection at every save;
- provider unavailable with durable retry state;
- new skill and social experiences during outage;
- a verified external journal exactly one record ahead;
- explicit forward-only adoption; and
- no duplicate/lost identity, event, perception, memory, pending-reflection, source-link, audit, or request records.

**Live RimWorld gate passed for the recorded scenario:** eight post-load snapshots across two normally terminated RimWorld processes used one copied save and one StoreId with provider dispatch paused. Four identity/lineage mappings remained stable, events and memories increased together from 111 to 117 with six unique new ID pairs, the two-item queue survived every load, and all stores remained healthy without Dagmay failure/recovery lines.

Exit criterion met for normal save/reload and shutdown:
no duplicate/lost identity or experience state, no silent branch mixing.

Forced termination, deliberate corruption/file locking, and explicit mismatch injection remain in 0.1M Failure Isolation.

## 0.1M — Failure Isolation

**Status:** Implemented, Compiled, and Tested in isolation; exit criterion met using deterministic fault injection plus existing live evidence.

`FailureIsolation` exercises unavailable/offline transport, invalid response, timeout, rate limit, provider error, cancellation, malformed nominal-success output, durable retry/quarantine, three interrupted-commit outcomes, storage mismatch, and shutdown/reload with pending work. It passes 103 assertions. Canonical bytes/state remain unchanged for every failure; the only permitted mutation is one fully revalidated absent-commit recovery. Already-applied recovery is idempotent, conflicting recovery is quarantined, and mismatched journal state is never auto-adopted.

Existing live evidence supplies the RimWorld half of the gate: 0.1L remained playable through two processes with a durable paused-provider queue, and 0.1J.3 exercised explicit checkpoint mismatch recovery. Malformed output and destructive persistence faults remain deterministic isolation fixtures rather than controls exposed against the owner's real stores.

Exit criterion met:
RimWorld remains playable and Dagmay fails closed without corrupting identity/history.
