# Version 0.1G — Ordinary Mind View and Disclosure Filtering

**Status:** Development candidate

## Purpose

Version 0.1G moves Dagmay from engineering-only inspection toward a normal user-facing view of an individual without weakening the Restricted Observer boundary.

The first 0.1G surface lives in **Options → Mod Settings → Dagmay**. Each enrolled individual has a **Mind** button that is available without unlocking Restricted Observer Mode.

## Ordinary view contract

The ordinary Mind view may show:

- display name and service state;
- a coarse natural-language summary derived from current affect;
- grounded identity facts already sourced from RimWorld; and
- memories explicitly classified `Shareable` and sufficiently accessible.

It must not show:

- `IndividualId` or `LineageId`;
- raw seven-dimensional affect values;
- provider/model diagnostics;
- reflection request or response payloads;
- private, relationship-sensitive, or Observer-only memories;
- hidden chain-of-thought; or
- administrative repair and persistence internals.

The projection is conservative by default. Version 0.1G classifies newly encoded skill gains and resolved health conditions as shareable. Critical-need experiences and newly added health conditions remain private. Existing 0.1F memories retain their stored privacy classification and are not retroactively rewritten.

## Why this is separate from Restricted Observer

The ordinary view is a disclosure-filtered projection intended to answer, "What could reasonably be surfaced about this individual?" Restricted Observer exists to answer, "What does the system actually store and why did it change?"

The same private records must never be passed to the ordinary renderer and merely hidden with visual controls. The RimWorld adapter constructs a separate ordinary projection containing only allowed fields.

## 0.1G verification plan

One owner-side integration session can verify several behaviors at once:

1. build and install 0.1G with the guarded development loop;
2. load the established colony and confirm all existing identity/lineage pairs survive;
3. confirm a new colonist appears in enrollment status without silently replacing an existing identity;
4. open **Mind** while Restricted Observer remains locked;
5. verify no IDs, raw affect numbers, private memories, or provider payloads appear;
6. generate a skill gain or health recovery and confirm a newly created shareable memory can appear in the ordinary view;
7. generate or retain a critical need/new health condition and confirm it remains absent from the ordinary view but visible in Restricted Observer after explicit unlock;
8. allow bounded Google reflection to continue and confirm ordinary rendering never exposes the raw validated reflection payload;
9. save, quit, reload, and repeat the Mind view check; and
10. collect diagnostics for build, storage, queue, request-budget, and continuity evidence.

## Scope boundary

0.1G remains Observer-only. It adds no action executor, work-priority change, job control, pathing influence, combat control, or pawn-behavior mutation.

A direct pawn inspect-tab or gizmo may be added later after the disclosure contract is stable. The first candidate intentionally reuses the existing Mod Settings route so the new privacy boundary can be tested without introducing an additional RimWorld patch surface.
