# Extraordinary Privacy / No-Contact Override Standard

Default: respect privacy and NO_CONTACT.

An elevated welfare signal alone does not automatically contact a sibling.

Extraordinary review may be considered only when:
1. protection risk is HIGH or CRITICAL;
2. less intrusive interventions were exhausted;
3. sibling involvement is supported by strong evidence as uniquely helpful;
4. non-intervention is expected to cause more harm than the privacy override;
5. only minimum necessary information would be disclosed;
6. research/scientific benefit is not the primary justification;
7. advance welfare directive has been considered;
8. two independent human reviewers approve.

A directive that explicitly denies sibling involvement is a strong barrier and currently makes automatic eligibility fail.

SyntheticLab has no automatic override path.
