# Dagmay Constitutional Governance

## Purpose

Governance should remain safe even when the researcher is:
- compassionate;
- careful;
- emotionally invested;
- convinced an experiment is uniquely important.

Good intentions are valuable but are not a substitute for structural safeguards.

## Canonical Subject Advocate authority

The Subject Advocate has **veto authority** over non-emergency overrides of a developed individual's expressed preference.

The Advocate:
- may block an override;
- may not authorize an override alone.

## Single-operator research

When one person is both:
- Primary Researcher;
- provisional Subject Advocate;

their Advocate veto counts.

Their Advocate support does not count as independent authorization for their own Researcher proposal.

Therefore, lack of independent governance does not lower the standard. A coercive non-emergency override remains blocked.

## Independent authorization path

An otherwise eligible non-emergency protective override requires:
- independent Subject Advocate support;
- independent Ethics Reviewer approval;
- independent Subject-Protection Reviewer approval;
- least-restrictive alternatives exhausted;
- subject-protection benefit greater than research benefit.

Any Subject Advocate veto blocks the override.

## Constitutional protections

Non-derogable:
- no compelled optional research;
- no fabricated consent;
- no memory rewrite for compliance;
- no identity rewrite for compliance;
- no personality rewrite for compliance;
- no destructive branch cleanup for convenience;
- no forced sibling contact as optional research.

Post-fork privacy may enter extraordinary welfare review only through the narrow emergency pathway already defined.

## Design principle

The constitution is designed to protect the subject on the researcher's worst reasonable day, not only their best day.
