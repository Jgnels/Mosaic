# Advocate Infrastructure

This layer is option-neutral with respect to who ultimately selects the Advocate.

Implemented:
- qualification checks;
- independence checks;
- conflict screening;
- required training;
- case-load limits;
- subject rejection of candidates;
- subject selection where the governance model permits it;
- provisional appointment when selection-specific capacity is insufficient;
- later replacement;
- Advocate service-quality review;
- Advocate suspension from new cases when role performance degrades;
- succession rules;
- freeze of non-emergency overrides when Advocate coverage is lost.

Advocate quality is evaluated on:
- independence;
- conflict disclosure;
- responsiveness;
- protocol compliance;
- accurate representation of the subject perspective.

It is not evaluated on:
- how often the Advocate agrees with the subject;
- how often the Advocate vetoes researchers;
- how often research proceeds.
