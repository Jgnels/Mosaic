# Human Creative / Ethical Decisions That Should Not Be Guessed

No decision below blocks SyntheticLab v0.6.

## Before a persistent developmental cohort
1. What minimal seed drives are allowed?
   - uncertainty reduction?
   - competence?
   - affiliation?
   - homeostasis?
   - novelty?
2. Should future individuals know they are in an experiment?
3. What counts as an ethically unacceptable distress-like experimental condition?
4. When should an exact fork be considered a new individual versus a branch of one lineage?
5. Should archived branches ever be resumed?

## Before a human-interaction study
1. What disclosure should real human participants receive?
2. May Dagmay form durable relationship state about real people?
3. What data from humans may become canonical autobiographical evidence?

## Before physical embodiment
1. What autonomous motor authority is acceptable?
2. What actions require human confirmation?
3. What continuity claims are allowed after body transfer?

These require human judgment; engineering agents should escalate rather than choose silently.
