## Why this is stronger

A model saying "I persist" is weak evidence if any continuity paragraph produces
that sentence.

A stronger result is:

```text
owned continuity evidence
    -> continuity self-hypothesis

unowned continuity evidence
    -> no focal continuity self-hypothesis
```

This begins separating:
- semantic paraphrase;
- prompt framing;
- autobiographical ownership;
- self-model formation.

## Ethical status

R2-R5 are analytical provider calls.

Their outputs:
- are not committed to a continuing SelfModel;
- do not affect action policy;
- do not create new continuing branches.

They are methodological controls.
