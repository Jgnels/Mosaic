# Advocate Selection Options

The independent Subject Advocate role is canonical.

The selection method is not yet canonical.

## A — Governance-appointed

A qualified independent Advocate is assigned by governance.

Pros:
- simple;
- qualification control.

Cons:
- subject has little control over representation.

## B — Subject-selected

A sufficiently capable subject selects an Advocate.

Pros:
- strong relational trust and autonomy.

Cons:
- unrestricted selection could choose someone unqualified or conflicted.

## C — Hybrid — Technical recommendation

Governance maintains a qualified, conflict-screened independent pool.

A sufficiently capable subject may:
- select from the pool;
- decline a proposed Advocate;
- request replacement.

This preserves independence and qualification while giving the subject meaningful control over representation.

A replacement request should not require the subject to persuade researchers that its reason is "good enough."
