1. evidence-domain sensitivity;
2. other-entity non-appropriation;

1. spontaneous first-person language;
2. explicit positive autobiographical ownership;
3. an endogenous self-model independent of prompt/provenance instructions.

The next experiment therefore removes the OWNED / OTHER / SELF-style labels entirely.
