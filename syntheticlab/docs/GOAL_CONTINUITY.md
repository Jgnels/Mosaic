# Goal Continuity and Emergency Interruption

Mosaic separates the committed ordinary goal from the currently active goal. A critical game need
temporarily interrupts the active goal without erasing the ordinary commitment. When the emergency
candidate disappears, the prior commitment receives its normal continuity advantage and can resume.

The offline longitudinal benchmark runs 256 seeded 200-tick histories. Two near-tied work goals
fluctuate in utility, with starvation emergencies at ticks 50, 100, and 150. It compares Mosaic's
commitment-aware selection with the same bounded scoring without memory. Acceptance requires at
least 70% fewer switches, at most 0.08 mean utility regret, perfect emergency response, and perfect
post-emergency commitment resumption.

This measures stable game behavior and player value. It does not represent desire, distress,
self-preservation, or consciousness.
