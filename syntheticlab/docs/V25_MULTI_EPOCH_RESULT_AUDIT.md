## Why it should not be treated as the final scientific interpretation

The same run also showed:

- mean confidence spread fell from `0.17` to `0.075`;
- all final branch trajectories endorsed cue-conditioned interaction;
- all final branch trajectories endorsed some form of contingent or predictive counterpart relation;
- the former one-way branch acquired stronger contingency claims;
- the former noncontingent branch acquired moderate contingency claims.

The free-text propositions also accumulated different wording and detail through four recursive LLM
rewrite steps.

Therefore increasing lexical distance may reflect **surface-language drift** even while important
belief dimensions converge.

## Current classification

`MEASUREMENT_CONFLICT`

We retain the preregistered lexical classification as a valid result of the chosen metric, but do not
promote it to the claim:

> the branches became semantically more psychologically divergent.

That stronger claim is not established.

## Resolution

v26 prepares an independent, blinded fixed-ontology semantic audit.

Only three starting propositions and six final propositions are evaluated.

The evaluator receives:
- one proposition at a time;
- an opaque item ID;
- no branch name;
- no stage label;
- no other propositions.

Six fixed dimensions are scored:

- cue actionability;
- contingent exchange;
- reciprocal influence;
- adaptive learning;
- temporal improvement;
- cross-counterpart generalization.

This audit is model-mediated and remains imperfect, but its measurement target is much closer to
semantic belief content than token overlap.
