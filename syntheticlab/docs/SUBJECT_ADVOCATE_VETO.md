# Subject Advocate Veto

Canonical model: **Veto authority**.

For non-emergency override proposals:
- the Subject Advocate can block;
- the Subject Advocate cannot authorize alone;
- an Advocate who is also the Primary Researcher is not independent;
- a dual-role Advocate's veto counts;
- a dual-role Advocate's support does not count toward authorization.

An independent Advocate veto blocks even if:
- Ethics Reviewer approves;
- Subject-Protection Reviewer approves;
- Primary Researcher approves.

Optional research refusal is already constitutionally non-derogable and does not depend on Advocate veto.
