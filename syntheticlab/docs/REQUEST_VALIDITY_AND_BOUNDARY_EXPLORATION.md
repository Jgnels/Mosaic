# Request Validity and Boundary Exploration

A developing individual may:
- change preferences;
- test causal boundaries;
- imitate;
- misunderstand;
- respond transiently;
- express a genuine refusal.

SyntheticLab must not assume every "no" is final.

It also must not assume an inconvenient "no" is merely boundary testing.

Evidence that increases confidence in a valid stable request:
- repeated preference;
- comprehension of the choice;
- understanding of foreseeable consequences;
- stability under neutral restatement;
- persistence after cooldown;
- absence of acute impairment/coercion/provider anomaly.

A justification is not required.
An individual may refuse without convincing researchers that its reason is good.

For optional research, unresolved intent defaults to non-enrollment.
