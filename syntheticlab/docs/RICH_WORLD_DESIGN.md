# Dagmay Rich Synthetic World

## Purpose

The Rich Synthetic World is the middle research layer between:
- sterile causal microbenchmarks;
- messy ecological environments such as RimWorld;
- future richer simulation or physical embodiment.

It exists because a mechanism can succeed in a tiny benchmark yet fail when:
- information is incomplete;
- causal rules change;
- resources deplete;
- multiple needs compete;
- other agents are unreliable;
- consequences unfold over long histories.

## Environment ladder

### MICRO_PLUS
Low complexity.
Stationary.
No social contingency.
Best for causal isolation.

### MESO
Partial observation.
Some social interactions.
Limited regime change.

### RICH
Longer developmental histories.
Competing energy/integrity needs.
Social advice and reciprocity.
Exact forks.

### RICH_HARD
Aliased environmental cues.
Repeated nonstationary causal regimes.
Resource depletion and regeneration.
Changing partner reliability.
Hidden truth used only for evaluation.
Exact JSON persistence/restart.

## Information architecture

Canonical world truth is separated from subject-visible perception.

The subject can receive:
- observed zone token;
- ambiguous regime cue;
- coarse internal state;
- noisy local signal;
- available partners;
- experienced action consequences;
- messages/hints.

The subject cannot receive:
- hidden world regime;
- canonical causal mapping;
- objective hint truthfulness;
- hidden partner profile parameters;
- exact hidden resource stock.

The research harness retains hidden truth for scoring.

## Why both simple and rich environments remain

Simple worlds answer:
> Did mechanism X cause effect Y?

Rich worlds answer:
> Does mechanism X remain useful when embedded in a complicated developmental history?

Neither answer substitutes for the other.
