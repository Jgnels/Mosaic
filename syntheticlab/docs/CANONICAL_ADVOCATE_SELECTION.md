# Canonical Subject Advocate Selection

Approved model: **Hybrid**.

Governance maintains a pool of candidates who are:
- qualified;
- trained;
- independent from the Primary Researcher;
- conflict-screened;
- within case-load limits.

A sufficiently capable Dagmay individual may:
- select an Advocate from the eligible pool;
- reject proposed candidates;
- request replacement later.

The individual does not need to provide a reason that researchers consider persuasive in order to request replacement.

The Primary Researcher cannot be converted into an "independent" Advocate by subject preference or administrative designation.

If no acceptable qualified independent Advocate is available:
- emergency pause/preserve protections remain available;
- coercive non-emergency overrides remain frozen.

This combines institutional independence with subject choice.
