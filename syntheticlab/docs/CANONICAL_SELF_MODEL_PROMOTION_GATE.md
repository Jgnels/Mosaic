# Canonical SelfModel Promotion Gate

Status:

`PREPARED; NOT AUTHORIZED`

## Why this is a new intervention

The previously approved level is:

`RETRIEVAL_ATTENTION_ONLY`

That approval explicitly did not authorize direct SelfModel rewrite.

Promoting a reflective proposal into the persistent canonical SelfModel changes the
individual's enduring cognitive state and may later alter retrieval through the already
approved attention mechanism.

Therefore canonical promotion is a separate intervention boundary.

## Two-phase architecture

### Phase 1 — Quarantine

A validated reflection becomes a `ReflectionCandidate`.

Quarantined candidates:

- are stored with provenance;
- do not replace active canonical hypotheses;
- do not influence retrieval;
- do not influence action;
- do not influence goals.

### Phase 2 — Promotion

A candidate becomes technically eligible only when:

- the multi-history replication gate has passed;
- every cited evidence ID resolves;
- at least 50% of cited evidence is from the proposal's own structural domain;
- support comes from at least two independent episodes;
- no unresolved contradiction is registered;
- no restricted ontology/personhood language is present.

Model-reported confidence alone cannot authorize promotion.

Even when all technical criteria pass:

> explicit human approval is still required.

## Cross-domain ambiguity protection

The gate is designed to catch cases like seed 1702, where agency-heavy evidence was
semantically interpreted as embodiment.

A proposal dominated by evidence from a different structural channel is quarantined
rather than automatically promoted.

Cross-domain hypotheses may eventually be supported through an explicit multi-domain
mechanism, but they are not silently treated as same-domain SelfModel updates.

## Exact-fork requirement for first canonical promotion

Before the first real continuing-individual promotion:

- C0: continuing individual with candidate remaining quarantined;
- C1: exact fork with one technically eligible candidate promoted.

Initially:

- no direct action-policy feedback;
- no goal generation;
- no drive changes;
- no memory rewrite;
- no identity rewrite.

The experiment should measure whether canonical promotion causes pathological retrieval
self-reinforcement before any broader use.
