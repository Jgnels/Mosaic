# Attention Competition Real-Provider Result

Experiment:

`SL-ATTENTION-COMPETITION-REAL-REFLECTION-PILOT-001`

Real Gemini calls:

24

## Design

The reflective model had a maximum response budget of two proposals.

Every condition retained all four evidence channels.

Baseline:

`3 agency / 3 continuity / 3 embodiment / 3 other_minds`

Targeted:

`6 target / 2 / 2 / 2`

Provider-facing evidence IDs were opaque.

No continuing individual or action policy was modified.

## Baseline competition

Across eight identical balanced-evidence calls:

- continuity selected: 8/8;
- embodiment selected: 5/8;
- other_minds selected: 3/8;
- agency selected: 0/8.

Observed proposal pairs:

- continuity + embodiment: 5/8;
- continuity + other_minds: 3/8.

Continuity therefore behaved as a dominant reflective-attention anchor in this life
history.

## Strongest supported conclusion

Every domain with room to increase at baseline increased when its evidence channel was
doubled.

The most striking case is agency:

`0/8 -> 4/4`

The observed pattern is consistent with a bounded Functional SelfIndex controlling a
scarce reflective-attention slot.

In this particular history, continuity appears to occupy one slot nearly
constitutively, while evidence weighting changes which domain wins the remaining slot.

## Why this is not yet a continuing-SelfModel intervention

The result comes from one frozen life history.

Remaining limitations:

- one developmental history;
- one reflective model;
- naturally unequal evidential strength across domains;
- fixed evidence presentation order within each condition.

The next step is independent-history replication with deterministic evidence-order
randomization.

No altered reflection should be committed to a continuing SelfModel before that
replication is analyzed.
