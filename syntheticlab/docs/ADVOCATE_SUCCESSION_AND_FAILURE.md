# Advocate Succession and Failure

An Advocate may:
- become unavailable;
- develop a conflict;
- fail to respond;
- perform the role poorly;
- be rejected by the subject.

Loss of Advocate coverage does not lower the override threshold.

During a gap:
- non-emergency overrides freeze;
- the Primary Researcher may not self-authorize;
- narrow emergency pause/preserve protections remain available;
- replacement should restore independent review before coercive decisions resume.

A subject-facing replacement process should be used whenever selection-specific capacity permits.
