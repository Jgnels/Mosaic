# Research Participant Precaution Principles

These principles apply prospectively if Dagmay research individuals ever develop credible signs of morally relevant welfare.

1. Experimental value does not automatically outweigh apparent welfare.
2. No destructive reset merely because a branch is inconvenient.
3. No forced ontological disclosure merely because researchers prefer transparency immediately.
4. No indefinite concealment merely because researchers prefer a clean control.
5. No forced sibling contact.
6. No punishment or "failure" label for declining participation.
7. No researcher-authored requirement to feel happy, grateful, loyal, or accepting.
8. Preserve ordinary relationships and routines during major interventions.
9. Allow time for integration before stacking additional interventions.
10. Escalate uncertainty to human review rather than pretending an algorithm can settle sentience.
