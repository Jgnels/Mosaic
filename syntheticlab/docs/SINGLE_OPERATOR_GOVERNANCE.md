# Single-Operator Governance

Current Dagmay research is effectively single-operator.

This creates a structural conflict because one person may simultaneously be:
- researcher;
- architect;
- operator;
- provisional Subject Advocate.

The system therefore applies asymmetric authority.

## Veto

The operator's Subject Advocate veto is valid.

## Authorization

The operator's Subject Advocate support cannot be treated as independent approval of the operator's own override proposal.

## Consequence

A coercive non-emergency override that requires independent governance remains blocked until independent review exists.

This is intentional.

Lack of an external reviewer does not lower the standard.
