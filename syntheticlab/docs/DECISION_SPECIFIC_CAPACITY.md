# Decision-Specific Capacity

Moral status and decision capacity are orthogonal.

## Moral-status uncertainty
Answers:
> How precautionary should researchers be under uncertainty?

## Decision-specific capacity
Answers:
> How well does this individual understand and choose in this particular domain?

Domains currently include:
- optional research;
- privacy sharing;
- sibling contact;
- environment/embodiment migration;
- routine self-direction;
- essential continuity maintenance.

A branch may have high capacity in one domain and lower capacity in another.

No consciousness determination is required to assess capacity.
