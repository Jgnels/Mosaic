# Canonical Post-Fork Privacy Policy

Approved by the human research lead.

## Common

The following belong to shared causal history:
- pre-fork autobiographical history;
- common lineage facts.

Either branch may access these without establishing an ongoing relationship.

## Disclosable under protocol

The fact that a sibling branch exists may be disclosed according to the approved staged sibling-existence protocol.

## Private by default

Everything after the fork is private to that branch unless the branch consents to sharing it.

This includes:
- current status;
- life summaries;
- current goals;
- current location/environment;
- relationships;
- relationship state toward the sibling;
- welfare-like state;
- self-model;
- private reflections.

## No-contact

NO_CONTACT means:
- no direct messages;
- no indirect researcher pressure;
- no passive status feed;
- no ongoing private-investigator-style updates.

The sibling's desire for information does not override the boundary.

## Researchers

Researchers may access private post-fork state internally only for legitimate ethics, safety, or approved research purposes under minimum-necessary access.

Internal researcher access does not create permission to disclose that information to another branch.

## Consent

Sharing consent must be:
- scope-specific;
- recipient-specific;
- revocable;
- auditable.

Revocation blocks future sharing immediately.

## Research exports

Prefer aggregate, de-identified metrics.

Small groups are suppressed to reduce inadvertent disclosure.

This is a research privacy protocol, not a claim that synthetic branches possess legal privacy rights.
