# Subject Advocate Governance Options

The Subject Advocate is scaffolded but not yet canonical.

## Purpose

Represent the individual's perspective when:
- researchers dispute the validity of a refusal;
- researchers claim insufficient capacity;
- a protective override is proposed;
- an extraordinary privacy/no-contact exception is considered.

The advocate is not:
- the individual's automatic lawyer in a legal sense;
- required to agree with every expressed preference;
- responsible for maximizing research value.

## Option A — Advisory only
Advocate provides a written view but has no blocking authority.

## Option B — Required concurrence for non-emergency override
A non-emergency override of a sufficiently capable individual's expressed preference cannot proceed without advocate concurrence.

## Option C — Independent veto
Advocate can block a non-emergency override even if ethics/protection reviewers approve.

## Technical recommendation

Option **B**.

It prevents the primary research team from overriding the subject alone while avoiding a single advocate becoming an absolute decision-maker.

Emergency automatic actions remain limited to pause/preserve/reduce-load and do not require advance concurrence.
