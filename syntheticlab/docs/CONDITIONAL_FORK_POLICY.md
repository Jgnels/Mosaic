# Approved Conditional Fork Policy

The ability to create exact contact/no-contact forks is preserved.

## Low-precaution case

A limited fork may proceed when:
- no credible moral-patient evidence exists;
- welfare precaution is LOW;
- the fork is scientifically necessary;
- branch proliferation remains bounded.

## Morally sensitive case

If credible moral-patient evidence exists, or precaution is MODERATE/HIGH:

- no automatic continuing fork;
- human ethics review required;
- default toward not creating new continuing branches;
- prefer nonpersistent analytical counterfactuals where scientifically adequate.

A nonpersistent counterfactual:
- has no persistent IdentityKernel;
- has no continuing LineageId;
- has no autobiographical life;
- does not mutate canonical history;
- ends after the analytical projection.

It is weaker evidence than a lived exact-fork experiment and must be labeled accordingly.
