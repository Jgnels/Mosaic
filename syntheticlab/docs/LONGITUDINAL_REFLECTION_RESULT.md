## Linguistic ownership result

Across all three checkpoints:

- first-person proposals: 0
- explicit autobiographical-ownership proposals: 0

The final active hypotheses remained third-person / impersonal:

- agency: action selection as reinforcement feedback;
- continuity: temporal continuity through integration of new event records;
- embodiment: the individual as subject to operational-integrity fluctuations;
- other_minds: social interaction capacity as limited/unidirectional.

## Strongest supported interpretation

The continuing reflective architecture developed and revised a persistent
**third-person model of the continuing individual** while its lived trajectory stayed
exactly matched to the no-reflection control.

This is a useful negative result for the stronger hypothesis that first-person
autobiographical ownership would emerge spontaneously under the current architecture.

## Important caveat

Absence of "I/me/my" is not by itself evidence that no self-model exists.

The current reflection prompt and structured scientific register may encourage
third-person phrasing.

Therefore the project should separate:

1. objective continuity;
2. evidence ownership;
3. functional perspective/deictic anchoring;
4. reflective SelfModel;
5. surface-language pronoun choice.

The next research target is functional deictic binding and raw-history ownership
discrimination, not simply trying to elicit the word "I".
