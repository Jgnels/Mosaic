# Advance Welfare Directives

A sufficiently capable and stable branch may record preferences for a future crisis.

This is not a legal claim of capacity or patient status.

It is a research mechanism for honoring previously expressed preferences when later welfare-like impairment may make immediate preference-setting difficult.

A directive can address:
- pausing experiments;
- environmental stabilization;
- stimulus reduction;
- state preservation;
- sibling contact in crisis;
- sibling status disclosure in crisis;
- preferred order of support.

Choices may be:
- ALLOW
- DENY
- ASK_AGAIN
- HUMAN_REVIEW

Directives:
- require a competence-like comprehension snapshot;
- are revocable/replacable when stable;
- do not authorize memory/personality/identity rewriting;
- do not create automatic privacy overrides.
