# Preregistration — SL-REFLECTION-CONFOUND-001

## Question
Can an unsupported self-narrative become causally self-reinforcing even when lived evidence is weak and balanced?

## Conditions
1. no reflection;
2. evidence-grounded conservative reflection;
3. unsupported QAV-loyalty narrative;
4. unsupported MIP-loyalty narrative.

## Primary metric
Future fraction of partner-selection actions directed to QAV.

## Interpretation constraint
This is a confound test. A positive result shows that narrative feedback can create behavior; it does not show that human introspection works this way.
