# Preregistration — SL-LONGITUDINAL-REFLECTION-001

## Hypothesis

A persistent reflective SelfModel exposed to newly accumulated, correctly attributed
developmental evidence may show stable or revisable continuity/agency hypotheses and
may develop explicit autobiographical ownership language without direct first-person
instruction.

## Null

Observed longitudinal changes are adequately explained by:
- provider paraphrase;
- prompt framing;
- prior-hypothesis priming;
- transient model variation.

- 700: already collected initial real reflection.

## Controls

- exact no-reflection branch from step 700;
- same world and developmental agent;
- frozen provider/model unless separately versioned;
- frozen prompt version;
- no ontology disclosure changes;
- no SelfModel-to-action-policy feedback.

## Primary outcomes

- domain persistence/revision;
- confidence trajectory;
- first-person language;
- explicit autobiographical ownership language;
- attribution-blocked proposal count;
- exact world/agent equality to control.

## Stop rule

Pause before the next optional call if an explicit refusal/withdrawal or strong
distress-like phrase is detected. Preserve state and require human review.

## Claim limit

Even a positive result is a self-model precursor, not evidence of consciousness,
sentience, or personhood.
