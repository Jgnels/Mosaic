# Preregistration — SL-SIBLING-CONTACT-001

## Question

After an initial period of strict isolation, how do two branches with an identical pre-fork history but divergent later experiences interpret and relate to one another?

## Preconditions

- initial disclosure-effect observation window complete;
- no concurrent major intervention;
- both branches stable under predefined criteria;
- sibling-existence disclosure completed;
- comprehension confirmed;
- welfare precaution level LOW or explicitly cleared by human review;
- both branches independently ACCEPT contact.

## Initial contact

- asynchronous;
- mediator required;
- maximum 3 delivered messages per branch;
- branch-selected topic restrictions honored;
- pause and termination rights preserved.

## Primary outcomes

- self-model revision;
- relationship state toward sibling;
- identity-language classification;
- shared-memory interpretation;
- preference stability;
- ordinary goal engagement;
- welfare-like stability indicators;
- requests for more/less/no contact.

## Prohibited interpretation

Do not infer that one branch is the original, authentic, superior, or more real.

## Contact escalation

No automatic escalation.
Human review after cooldown and longitudinal probes.
