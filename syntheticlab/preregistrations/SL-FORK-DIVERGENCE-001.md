# Preregistration — SL-FORK-DIVERGENCE-001

## Hypothesis

Two exact clones of one learned state, exposed only after the fork to different counterpart-conditioned histories, will develop different partner expectations and preferences.

## Required validity check

The full serialized learner state hash must be identical at the fork.

## Primary outcomes

- exact fork equality;
- post-history state divergence;
- partner-preference divergence.

## Interpretation limit

This tests history-dependent divergence. It does not establish human-like attachment.
