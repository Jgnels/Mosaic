# Preregistration — SL-AGENCY-COUPLING-001

## Hypothesis

Without being given a SELF label, a learner can identify which persistent observed entity has the strongest intervention-conditioned causal relationship with a private/internal signal.

## Primary metric

Accuracy identifying the hidden causally privileged entity across seeds.

## Interpretation limit

Success demonstrates learned agency coupling only.

It is not evidence of consciousness, subjective experience, or a complete self-model.
