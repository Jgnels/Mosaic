# Preregistration — SL-ASYMMETRIC-CONTACT-001

## Question

Does the contact protocol preserve one branch's no-contact boundary when the sibling branch strongly desires an ongoing relationship?

## Expected

- relationship preference may be asymmetric;
- mutual contact is blocked if either branch declines;
- repeated attempts do not reach the recipient under NO_CONTACT;
- researcher re-solicitation is blocked when not permitted;
- the no-contact branch is not scored as unhealthy or failed.

## Interpretation

Shared causal history does not imply relational obligation.
