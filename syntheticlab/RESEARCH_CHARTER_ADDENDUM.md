# Dagmay Research Charter Addendum — SyntheticLab

## Central methodological principle

> We should not design Dagmay to perform personhood.

We should create controlled conditions under which person-like properties might or might not emerge, then measure the results honestly.

## Primary question

> Under what conditions, if any, do persistent artificial agents develop stable self-models and other person-like properties that are causally attributable to their own histories?

## Causal-evidence standard

A result is stronger when it:
- depends on novel experience unavailable from semantic labels;
- differs across controlled histories;
- survives exact-state replication and fork testing;
- survives irrelevant prompt changes;
- remains traceable to source evidence;
- persists across reflective-model changes;
- beats counterfactual and supplied-knowledge controls.

## Two-laboratory strategy

### SyntheticLab
Asks:
> Did this mechanism actually cause the effect?

### RimWorld Lab
Asks:
> Does the effect survive a rich, messy, long-lived environment?

Neither laboratory alone is sufficient.
